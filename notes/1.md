## Clarifying note: where is the "SOAR" in this lab?

- **TheHive** = case management / alert triage layer of SOAR
- **Cortex** = automation/orchestration layer (responders = simple one-shot playbooks, analyzers = enrichment)
- Together they form a **basic/baseline SOAR** already present in the lab
- Limitation: Cortex responders are simple one-shot actions, not multi-step conditional playbooks
- This is exactly why Phase 2/3 benchmarking matters — evaluating whether a dedicated SOAR engine (Shuffle, StackStorm, etc.) adds real value on top of / instead of TheHive+Cortex automation
